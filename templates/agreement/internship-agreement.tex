The Intern will assist with projects and responsibilities related to
\textbf{\dkPEngagementProjectDescription} and may be assigned other reasonable
tasks relevant to the internship.

\section*{2. Nature of the Internship}
The internship is intended to provide practical experience, mentorship, learning,
and exposure to real-world projects. This internship is temporary and does not
guarantee employment with Paridyum during or after the internship.

\section*{3. Compensation}
This is an \textbf{unpaid internship}. No salary, wages, stipend, bonus, or other
financial compensation is promised under this Agreement.

\ifdkPEducationCurrentlyEnrolled
This Agreement is made on the basis that the Intern is currently enrolled as a
student at \textbf{\dkPEducationInstitution} in the \dkPEducationDegree{} programme,
that the internship is undertaken as part of the Intern's education, and that it is
limited to the fixed period stated in section 1.
\else
\textbf{[Unpaid basis not evidenced: no current enrolment recorded. This document
must not be issued in this state --- see registry and DECISION-DEBT.md D-5.]}
\fi

Where applicable, Paridyum may provide reasonable documentation required by the
Intern's university or educational institution for internship completion or academic
credit. Nothing in this Agreement is intended to remove any right or entitlement that
cannot legally be waived.

\section*{4. Responsibilities}
The Intern agrees to:
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=*, noitemsep]
    \item perform assigned work responsibly and professionally;
    \item communicate progress and blockers to their supervisor;
    \item follow reasonable instructions related to assigned projects;
    \item protect confidential information;
    \item follow Paridyum's information-security requirements;
    \item use Company systems and accounts only for authorized purposes;
    \item respect intellectual-property and third-party rights; and
    \item promptly report security incidents, accidental disclosures, or lost
          credentials.
\end{itemize}

\section*{5. Confidentiality}
During the internship, the Intern may receive access to confidential information
relating to Paridyum, including source code, product plans, designs, business
information, technical documentation, internal communications, and other non-public
information. The Intern agrees to comply with the separate
\textbf{Non-Disclosure Agreement} signed as part of the internship documentation.

\section*{6. Intellectual Property}
Work created specifically by the Intern as part of assigned Paridyum projects will be
governed by the separate \textbf{Intellectual Property Assignment Agreement} signed by
the Intern. The Intern must not knowingly use confidential information, proprietary
code, or other protected material belonging to a previous employer, university,
client, or third party in Paridyum projects without proper authorization.

\section*{7. Company Systems and Security}
Any access provided to Paridyum repositories, accounts, infrastructure, documents, or
other systems is provided only for authorized internship work. The Intern must:
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=*, noitemsep]
    \item keep passwords and credentials private;
    \item use multi-factor authentication where required;
    \item not share Company accounts;
    \item not access systems unrelated to assigned work;
    \item not publish private source code or internal documents;
    \item not expose passwords, API keys, or other secrets; and
    \item follow the \textbf{Information Security \& Acceptable Use Policy}.
\end{itemize}

\section*{8. Use of AI and Third-Party Tools}
The Intern must not upload Paridyum's confidential information, private source code,
credentials, customer information, or unreleased product information to external AI
tools or other third-party services unless specifically authorized. Open-source
software and third-party materials may only be used in accordance with their
applicable licenses and Paridyum's instructions.

\section*{9. No Authority to Represent Paridyum}
The Intern is not authorized to enter into contracts, make financial commitments, make
public statements on behalf of Paridyum, or otherwise legally bind Paridyum unless
specifically authorized in writing.

\section*{10. Portfolio and Public Sharing}
The Intern may mention their internship with Paridyum on their resume, CV, or
professional profile. However, confidential project information, private source code,
internal screenshots, unreleased products, customer information, or proprietary
documents may not be publicly shared without prior written permission.

\section*{11. Ending the Internship}
Either Paridyum or the Intern may end the internship by providing
\textbf{\dkOrgNoticePeriodDays{} days' written notice}. Paridyum may end the internship
immediately in cases involving serious misconduct, confidentiality violations,
unauthorized system access, fraud, intentional security violations, or other serious
breaches of this Agreement.

\section*{12. Offboarding}
When the internship ends, the Intern must:
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=*, noitemsep]
    \item stop accessing Paridyum systems;
    \item return Company equipment and materials;
    \item return or delete confidential information when requested;
    \item transfer unfinished work and relevant documentation; and
    \item cooperate with reasonable knowledge-transfer and account-removal procedures.
\end{itemize}
Confidentiality and intellectual-property obligations that are intended to continue
after the internship will remain effective.

\section*{13. Future Incorporation of Paridyum}
The Intern understands that Paridyum is currently being operated by
\textbf{\dkSigDirectorName} (\dkSigDirectorDesignation) and may later be operated
through a registered company. Paridyum may transfer this Agreement and related
business rights to a future legal entity established to operate Paridyum, where
permitted by law. Any intellectual-property transfer will remain governed by the
separate Intellectual Property Assignment Agreement.

\section*{14. Governing Law}
This Agreement will be governed by the laws of \dkOrgJurisdictionCountry. Subject to
applicable law, disputes relating to this Agreement will be subject to the appropriate
courts in \textbf{\dkOrgJurisdictionForum}.

\section*{15. Acceptance}
By signing below, both parties confirm that they have read and understood this
Agreement and agree to its terms.
