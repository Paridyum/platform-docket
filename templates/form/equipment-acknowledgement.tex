\section*{3. Intern Responsibilities}
The Intern agrees to:
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=*, noitemsep]
    \item use the equipment responsibly and primarily for authorized Paridyum work;
    \item take reasonable care to prevent loss, theft, or damage;
    \item not sell, lend, transfer, or give the equipment to another person;
    \item not intentionally disable required security features;
    \item promptly report loss, theft, serious damage, or security concerns; and
    \item return the equipment when requested or when the internship ends.
\end{itemize}
Normal wear and tear from reasonable use will not be treated as damage.

\section*{4. Security}
Where applicable, the Intern must:
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=*, noitemsep]
    \item keep the device protected with a password, PIN, or other authentication;
    \item follow Paridyum's Information Security \& Acceptable Use Policy;
    \item protect Company accounts and credentials; and
    \item avoid installing unsafe or unauthorized software.
\end{itemize}
