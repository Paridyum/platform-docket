\section*{3. What Paridyum Will Process, and Why}
Paridyum will process the Intern's name, contact details, education details,
internship details, and records of the documents issued to and signed by them, for
purposes connected with the internship: arranging and administering it, issuing and
managing system access, preparing the internship documents, providing documentation
required by the Intern's institution, and meeting Paridyum's own record-keeping
obligations. Full details are set out in the Data Protection Notice, which is
provided with this form.

\vspace{0.5em}
\begin{infobox}
\textbf{What Paridyum will not do}\\[0.5em]
Paridyum does not and will not undertake tracking or behavioural monitoring of the
Intern, and does not direct advertising at them. These are prohibited outright where
a person is under eighteen, and consent does not make them permissible.

\vspace{0.4em}
Paridyum does not collect the Intern's financial account details, government
identity numbers, or health records for this internship.
\end{infobox}
