\section*{3. What Paridyum Will Not Do}
Paridyum will not:
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=*, noitemsep]
    \item alter a photograph in a way that misrepresents you;
    \item imply you endorse a product or claim you have not endorsed;
    \item pass your image or quotation to another organisation for their own
          marketing; or
    \item use any of this after you withdraw consent, except where it has
          already been printed or published and cannot reasonably be recalled.
\end{itemize}

\section*{4. Withdrawing}
Write to \textbf{\dkOrgEmail} at any time, during the internship or after it. No
reason is needed. Paridyum will stop using the material and remove it from
anything it controls within a reasonable period. Material already in print, or
copied by others beyond Paridyum's control, may persist --- which is the honest
limit of what withdrawal can achieve, and the reason to consider this before
consenting rather than after.

\section*{5. No Payment}
No payment is made for this consent, and none is owed.
