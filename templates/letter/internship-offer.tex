\section*{1. Nature of the Internship}
This internship is intended to give you practical experience, mentorship and exposure
to real projects. It is temporary, and it does not guarantee employment with Paridyum
during or after the internship.

\section*{2. Compensation}
\textbf{This is an unpaid internship.} No salary, wages, stipend, bonus or other
financial compensation is offered.

\ifdkPEducationCurrentlyEnrolled
This offer is made on the basis that you are currently enrolled as a student at
\textbf{\dkPEducationInstitution}, that the internship forms part of your education,
and that it runs for the fixed period stated above. Please tell us before you accept
if any of that is not correct, as it changes the basis of this offer.
\else
\textbf{[This offer cannot be issued: no current enrolment is recorded, and the
unpaid basis rests on it. See DECISION-DEBT.md D-5.]}
\fi

Where you need it, we will provide reasonable documentation for your institution for
internship completion or academic credit.

\section*{3. Documents to Be Signed}
If you accept, the internship is subject to your signing the following before your
start date:
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=*, noitemsep]
    \item Internship Agreement;
    \item Non-Disclosure Agreement;
    \item Intellectual Property Assignment Agreement;
    \item Information Security \& Acceptable Use Policy;
    \item Policy on Prevention of Sexual Harassment (POSH); and
    \item Data Protection Notice.
\end{itemize}
You will also be asked to complete the Intern Onboarding Form and the Emergency
Contact Form. Please read each document before signing, and ask us about anything that
is not clear.

\section*{4. Ending the Internship}
Either you or Paridyum may end the internship on
\textbf{\dkOrgNoticePeriodDays{} days' written notice}, as set out in the Internship
Agreement.
